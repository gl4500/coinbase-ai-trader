§ 3 — forking paths

Three honest moves remain.
The cache is the constraint, not the booster.

A

Relax the gate.

Drop production threshold from 0.55 to a level the 0.5199 baseline can clear after calibration. Ship XGB as the live backend at 0.52–0.53 AUC, post-isotonic. Treat 0.55 as advisory.

  • Lowest cost — no retrain, no new data
  • Risk: ensemble overfit on marginal lift
  • Calibrator already monotone (Session 58.11)
cost cheap · ship-ready
B

Bring in new input classes.

Stop sourcing transforms from the same OHLC + volume cache. Add input modalities the booster has never seen — full per-product OKX OI panel, options term structure, on-chain flow.

  • OKX OI panel · expanding existing Ch 27
  • Options surface · IV/RV term structure
  • On-chain · Glassnode / Nansen tier
cost data subscription + 1–2 retrain cycles
C

Park XGB at 0.528.

Accept that the 28-channel cache caps out near 0.53 AUC and treat the XGB cutover as a plateau. Keep CNN at threshold 0.99 (sidelined) and let the live shadow loop accumulate evidence before the next move.

  • Zero engineering — pure observation
  • Costs market exposure of the gap
  • Default if A & B both decline
cost opportunity · not capital

What the six failures rule out, together.

At the 0.5199 baseline + ±0.005 fold-noise, neither positioning (volume / dominance / L-S ratios), long-horizon trend (SMA50/200, golden cross), nor cross-asset temporal (BTC lag, β, β-residual) features clear the +0.01 gate on the 28-channel survivorship-aware top-20 cache.

That makes the conclusion sharper than "XGB is hard to tune." The production 0.55 gate is not reachable on price + orderflow features alone at this sample regime. Tuning is exhausted; further lift requires either a different gate or a different feature modality.
