Win-Factors Readout · The #1 Lever

The edge exists.
The fees eat it.

−$414 → +$169

Across 1,932 paper entries the strategy is +0.60% profitable per trade before fees — 67% of trades close green gross. The 1.2% taker round-trip is bigger than that edge, so it gets donated to the exchange. Pay the lower maker fee instead and the same trades flip from a $414 loss to a $169 gain. The signal was never the problem. The toll booth is.

Mechanics

Two ways to fill an order

Every trade pays a fee on the way in and the way out. Which fee depends on how you fill — not what you trade.

◤ Taker — crossing

~0.60%/side
1.20% round-trip · what runs live today

A market order reaches across the spread and fills instantly against a resting order. Certain, immediate — and expensive. This is what execute_signal does on every entry right now.

ASK 100.06 · 1.4
YOU BUY → cross ↑
— spread —
BID 100.00 · 2.1

◢ Maker — resting

~0.20%/side
0.40% round-trip · 3× cheaper

A post-only limit sits on the book at the bid (buy) / ask (sell) and waits for someone to fill you. Cheaper by 3× — but the fill isn't guaranteed; price can walk away first. This is execute_maker_signal.

— spread —
YOU BID 100.00 — rest & wait
BID 99.98 · 3.0

Reality Check · the code

What's built vs. what isn't

Reading the actual source corrected the easy story. The maker function is real — but it's unwired and entry-only.

✓ Built

execute_maker_signal()

Real & tested at order_executor.py:285. Posts a post-only LIMIT at bid/ask, polls 30s for a fill, and market-falls-back if it doesn't fill so the entry is never lost. test_order_executor_maker.py covers it.

⚠ Unwired

The live path still calls the taker

Both cnn_agent.py:2206 and main.py:1032 call execute_signal (taker/market). The maker function's own docstring: "Purely additive — no caller is migrated until the user opts in." Step one is flipping the caller — on the shadow backend only.

+ New work

Maker-routed profit exits

execute_maker_signal is entry-only. The exit paths (exit_watcher, _check_risk_exits) fire their own sells. Routing profit-target exits through maker while keeping disaster stops on taker is genuinely new wiring — the bigger half of the job.

Why it needs your go-ahead

Fill rates are a live property — they can't be backtested

The +$169 assumes maker orders fill. In reality an entry may miss (selectively, on fast movers) and a resting profit-limit may not fill in a sharp move. The only way to learn the real numbers is to run it and measure — on a shadow port, never on live.
