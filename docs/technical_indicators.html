V. Categories — how they relate

◀ Trend / direction

"Is there a trend and which way?"

  • SMA — baseline smoothing, equal weight
  • EMA — faster smoothing, recent weight
  • MACD — difference of two EMAs (momentum of trend)
  • ADX / DMI — strength of trend, not direction
  • Parabolic SAR — trailing reversal stop
  • Ichimoku — trend + S/R + momentum in one cloud

◀ Momentum / oscillator

"Is the move stretched? Bounded 0–100 or zero-centered."

  • RSI — smoothed gain/loss ratio, bounded 0–100
  • Stoch RSI — RSI inside its own range, even more reactive
  • CCI — deviation from typical price, unbounded
  • Williams %R — inverted Stoch, bounded -100–0
  • ROC — raw % change over N bars, zero-centered

◀ Volatility

"How wide is the move?"

  • Bollinger Bands — SMA ± k·σ envelope
  • ATR — mean true range, raw $ volatility

◀ Volume confirmation

"Is the move backed by participation?"

  • VWAP — volume-weighted average price (fair value)
  • OBV — cumulative signed volume (accumulation/distribution)
  • CMF — volume-weighted close position within bar

VI. How to use them — common patterns

Pattern Combo What it tells you
Trend confirmation EMA(20) + EMA(50) + ADX > 25 Faster EMA above slower with ADX rising = a real trend (not chop). Trade with EMA direction; ADX < 20 → step aside.
Mean-reversion entry Bollinger lower band + RSI < 30 Price tags lower band AND oscillator is oversold. Higher quality than either alone — Bollinger gives the price location, RSI confirms the stretch.
Breakout filter Bollinger squeeze → expansion + OBV uptick Volatility contraction (narrow bands) followed by an expansion; OBV confirms volume is behind the breakout, not a fake-out.
Trend exhaustion Price new high + RSI/MACD lower high (divergence) Momentum is fading while price still climbs. Classic warning, not an entry — wait for a structural break to act.
Volatility-based stop Entry − 2·ATR  (or Parabolic SAR) Stops sized to current volatility avoid getting bumped by normal noise; ATR retunes per-bar. SAR does the same as a trailing dot.
Fair-value rotation VWAP ± 1σ bands (intraday) Institutions use VWAP as the execution benchmark. Fades back toward VWAP work in range-bound sessions; trend sessions ride one side.
Cloud trend filter Price > Ichimoku cloud + bullish Kumo Ichimoku rolls trend + S/R + momentum into one chart object. Above cloud + green cloud ahead = bullish regime; trade longs only.
Volume-confirmed accumulation CMF > 0 + OBV rising + price consolidating Smart money loading the boat under a flat tape. Often precedes a breakout the other indicators only confirm afterward.
Oscillator stacking RSI + Stoch RSI agreement Stoch RSI fires far more signals than RSI; requiring RSI to corroborate cuts the false-positive rate by ~⅔.
Rate-of-change filter ROC sign change + price-cross MA ROC = momentum's first derivative. Pairs with MA crosses as a "is the cross actually accelerating?" check.
